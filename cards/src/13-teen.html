HOW TO SHOVEL
SOMERVILLE, MA

Need a
shoveler?

The City pairs paid teens with neighbors who can't shovel.
1
Who it's for
Seniors and people with disabilities or medical conditions.
2
Sign up before it snows
Call 617-625-6600 x2321, or go to 167 Holland St with a photo ID.
3
What it costs
$25 for sidewalk and steps, $60 for a corner lot. 2024/25 rates.
4
Teens 14 to 19 get paid
To shovel, call 617-625-6600 x2406.
