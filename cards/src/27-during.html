SNOW EMERGENCY PARKING
SOMERVILLE, MA

Snow
emergency?
Move your car.

The City announces it with flashing blue lights mounted at 22 major intersections, and by City Alert.
You then have 4 hours to park on the even-numbered side of the street (winter 2026/27).
The empty side lets plows clear the street for buses and emergency vehicles. Parked on the wrong side? A $100 ticket, and you may be towed.
