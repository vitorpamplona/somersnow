Riding transit in Somerville

Weekday boardings at Somerville's bus stops and at the T stations its people use, and Bluebikes trips from its docks, every fall since 2016. From the MBTA's counts and Bluebikes' trip data.

By the Somerville Pedestrian & Transit Advisory Committee (PTAC). Not an MBTA or City page.

A decade of boardings

Each weekday, every fall. Buses at stops inside Somerville, plus the T stations: Davis, Assembly and the five new Green Line stations in the city, and Porter and Sullivan Square just over the line.

The MBTA has no T station counts for Fall 2016 or Fall 2020–2022, so those years show buses only. A rider who changes from a bus to the T is counted twice.

Bluebikes

Trips starting at Bluebikes docks in Somerville, each weekday, September to November.

Fall here is September to November, holidays left out. More docks bring more trips: Somerville had 12 docks with trips in 2016–17, 24 in 2018 and 36–39 since 2022. From 2024 the files mark e-bikes: about 30% of these trips.

All the numbers

How it's counted

  • Buses: the MBTA's average boardings per scheduled trip and stop, each fall, added up for every weekday trip at stops inside Somerville (each stop placed with that fall's schedule, then matched to the city's outline). Fall 2025 on, the MBTA reports Friday on its own; it's folded into a Monday–Friday average.
  • The T: the MBTA's average weekday boardings at each station (Fall 2017–2019 and Fall 2023–2025; it has published none for 2016 or 2020–2022). Porter (Cambridge) and Sullivan Square (Charlestown) are just outside the city but used by many in it; they also serve their own neighborhoods. Lechmere is left out: it's farther from the city line, next to industrial land rather than homes.
  • Bluebikes: every trip that starts at a dock inside Somerville, from Bluebikes' public trip files, averaged over the weekdays (and weekend days) of September to November, without Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and the Friday after. Each station is placed at its dock (from 2024, e-bike trips record the bike's GPS position, not the dock's). Trips, not boardings: one trip is one ride.
  • Counted twice: a rider who takes a bus to Davis or Sullivan and gets on the T there is counted at both. The totals count boardings, not people.
  • Somerville since 2015: the city's transit changed a lot (Assembly opened in 2014, the pandemic in 2020, the Green Line Extension in 2022, the bus network redesign from December 2024). Compare years with those breaks in mind.
  • An estimate: the MBTA's counts are averages from automatic passenger counters on a sample of trips, not every rider.

Sources: MBTA bus ridership by trip, season, route and stop; MBTA rail ridership by season, time period and stop (2017–2019) and the Fall 2023, Fall 2024 and Fall 2025 rail files; stop locations from the MBTA schedule archive; Bluebikes system data; town outlines: MassGIS.
