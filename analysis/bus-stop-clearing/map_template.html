    Bus stop routes

    Design the routes the bobcat crews take to clear bus stops after a storm. Pick a crew, then press on the map and drag across stops in the order that crew should clear them; or tap them one by one, or add them by name. The page plays the day and scores it: how soon riders get a clear stop, how far the crews drive, and where they jump across town. Make as many plans as you like, each with its own crews, and compare them. Your plans and settings stay in this browser.

    Settings

    Change any assumption and the scores update. Settings are saved in this browser.

    Save and share

    Your plans save in this browser as you go. To move them to another computer, or send them to someone, export a file or copy a link. Importing a file adds its plans to yours; opening a link replaces your plans and settings with the ones in the link.

    How the page works

      Made by the Somerville Pedestrian & Transit Advisory Committee (PTAC). Not an official City page; the City publishes neither its list of 281 stops nor its 4 routes. Sources: weekday boardings and alightings by stop, (MBTA open data); stops and routes (MBTA V3 API); route 109 cleared by the MBTA (MBTA: bus service during winter storms); 281 stops in 4 routes (City snow page); ward outlines (City GIS).
