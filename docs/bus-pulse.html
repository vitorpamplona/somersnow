How it's made

Every weekday trip on the 15 bus routes that stop in Somerville (), moving at its scheduled times along its route.

At each stop, riders getting on and off are the MBTA's average counts for that trip. Riders on board are what's left after each stop.

Trains: every weekday Red, Orange, Green and Blue Line trip on the map, at its scheduled times. Each station's riders getting on, off and riding through are the MBTA's average weekday counts for each hour, shared evenly among that hour's trains. Stations glow on a gentler scale than bus stops: they have far more riders.
