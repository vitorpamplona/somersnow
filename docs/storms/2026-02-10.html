Sources

  • Weather: hourly observations at Boston Logan (BOS), Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Snowfall: NOAA via ACIS.
  • Requests: Somerville 311 open data.
  • Wards: City of Somerville GIS. Bus riders: MBTA.
  • Warnings: National Weather Service, zone MAZ014, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet VTEC archive.
  • Tows and disabled vehicles: Somerville police dispatch data (2017 on, per 8-hour shift). Crashes: Somerville police crash reports (2018 on).
  • T entries: MBTA gated station entries at stations in Somerville. MBTA alerts: MBTA service alerts archive (2020 on). Bikes: Bluebikes trip data. Buses: MBTA bus arrival and departure times (2020 on), on the routes that served Somerville at the time: today's routes that ran then, plus any route with a timing stop in Somerville then (routes change: the 86 stopped in Somerville in 2020–22, not in 2026). "Normal" is the same hour on the 4 weeks before. Bus speed: each bus's time between two timing stops in Somerville, compared with that stretch at the same hour (weekdays and weekends apart) over the 4 weeks before; slow buses mean busy streets, snowbanks or bad roads, not a count of cars (no public data counts cars on Somerville streets).
  • Fire Department: National Fire Incident Reporting System (FEMA, U.S. Fire Administration; the "U.S. Fire Department Responses" public release), Somerville Fire Department, 2019–2025, by the type found on arrival. Medical calls are EMS calls and medical assists; the release doesn't say why someone needed help, so they are not a count of falls. Wires down includes arcing and electrical problems; carbon monoxide includes CO detectors that went off. Streets are shown without house numbers. No incidents were filed Feb 25–28 and Dec 10–15, 2022.
  • Card spending: Opportunity Insights Economic Tracker (Affinity Solutions card data), Middlesex County, seasonally adjusted, 7-day average, each day compared with the 4 weeks before. County-wide and smoothed, so a one-day closure looks smaller than it was; it is spending, not City revenue (the City publishes no daily revenue). Daily data covers 2020 to early 2022.
  • Who walks: Somerville Happiness Survey, "In the past week, which of the following transportation options did you use?" Melt and refreeze: hourly temperature at Logan; "refreezing" means back at or below 32°F within 12 hours after it was 34°F or warmer.
  • Snow in Somerville: CoCoRaHS volunteer observers inside Somerville, via ACIS (24 hours to about 7 a.m.). City page views: City of Somerville website analytics (top 20 pages per day, 2020 to May 2023).
  • Deadline: 6 daylight hours after the snow stops, Sec. 12-8(a), computed with the guide's calculator.
