THE SIDEWALK RULE
SOMERVILLE, MA

No snow
or ice into
the street

It refreezes there, or the plow pushes it back onto the sidewalk.
Where it goes: onto your own property, or hire a contractor to haul it away.
Only allowed in daylight on a day above 40°F, broken up and spread no more than 3 inches thick.
Breaking the rule costs the same as an uncleared sidewalk: $100 to $300.
