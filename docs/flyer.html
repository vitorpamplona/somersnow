Who shovels?

  • Owners and tenants: the sidewalk in front. Corner lot? Both sidewalks.
  • Landlords: shared exits, stairs and walkways. A lease can't change it.
  • Away? Still your job. Line up help.
  • Don't shovel snow into the street.
