HOW TO SHOVEL
SOMERVILLE, MA

Clear your
vents

Snow piled against the house can block exhaust vents and send carbon monoxide back inside.
1
Furnace and heater vents
Keep 2 to 3 feet clear around them, and around the gas meter.
2
Your car's tailpipe
Clear it before you start the engine.
3
Test your alarm
Carbon monoxide has no smell. A working detector is the only warning.
