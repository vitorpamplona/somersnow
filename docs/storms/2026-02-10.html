After the snow stops, sidewalk reports keep coming for 6 days in a typical storm. Where it snowed once, reports peaked 1 to 3 days after the snow in 20 of 23 storms.

Somerville PTAC

Not an official City page. The City's rules apply: somervillema.gov/snow. The guide's "storm mode" messages here are a sketch of what it could show; the City notices are quoted from the City's news posts. Source code and data.

Sources

  • Weather: Boston Logan, hourly (Iowa Environmental Mesonet). Snowfall: NOAA via ACIS; in Somerville, CoCoRaHS volunteers. Warnings: National Weather Service (archive).
  • 311 requests: Somerville open data. Tows: police dispatch (2017 on). Crashes: police crash reports (2018 on).
  • MBTA: station entries, bus times (2020 on), subway times (2022 on), alerts (2020 on), bus riders. Bikes: Bluebikes.
  • Fire Department calls: FEMA fire incident reports (2019–2025). Card spending: Opportunity Insights, Middlesex County (2020–22).
  • Wards: City GIS. Who walks: Happiness Survey. City page views: website analytics (2020–23).
  • Deadline: 6 daylight hours after the snow stops (Sec. 12-8(a)), from the guide's calculator.
  • Data: CSV · JSON · raw records · guide · code.
  • "Normal": the same hour, 4 weeks before. 311 counts complaints, not sidewalks. Medical calls aren't falls. Method notes.
