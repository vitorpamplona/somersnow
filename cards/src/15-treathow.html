HOW TO SHOVEL
SOMERVILLE, MA

How to use
ice melt

1
Start with a light scatter
A little ice melt as the storm starts keeps snow from bonding to the pavement.
2
Then shovel
Ice melt is for the thin layer left behind, not for melting a snowfall.
3
Use a coffee mug
About 12 ounces of salt covers 10 sidewalk squares. More does not melt faster.
4
Sweep up what is left
Leftover salt and sand wash into storm drains and rivers. Save them for next time.
