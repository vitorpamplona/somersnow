HOW TO SHOVEL
SOMERVILLE, MA

WATCH FOR HEART ATTACKS

Shoveling heavy snow can trigger a heart attack, the American Heart Association warns. Two minutes in, heart rates pass 85% of maximum.
1
Push, don't lift
Take small loads, and clear it in rounds before it piles up.
2
Take breaks
Chest pain, short of breath or dizzy? Stop and call 911.
3
Ask for help
If you have a heart condition or rarely exercise, let someone else shovel.
