SNOW EMERGENCY PARKING
SOMERVILLE, MA

Space
savers?

1
Not allowed
Somerville prohibits cones, chairs or anything else left in the street to hold a parking spot.
2
They get thrown out
The City can collect and dispose of them.
3
Why it's illegal
A $100 ticket. City law bars leaving objects in the street without a Public Works permit, and each day counts as a new violation.
4
Dig out your own
Don't take a spot someone else shoveled. Shovel your own, or find help.
