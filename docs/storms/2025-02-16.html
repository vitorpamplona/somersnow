Sources

  • Weather: hourly observations at Boston Logan (BOS), Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Snowfall: NOAA via ACIS.
  • Requests: Somerville 311 open data.
  • Wards: City of Somerville GIS. Bus riders: MBTA.
  • Warnings: National Weather Service, zone MAZ014, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet VTEC archive.
  • Tows and disabled vehicles: Somerville police dispatch data (2017 on, per 8-hour shift). Crashes: Somerville police crash reports (2018 on).
  • T entries: MBTA gated station entries at stations in Somerville. MBTA alerts: MBTA service alerts archive (2020 on). Bikes: Bluebikes trip data. Buses: MBTA bus arrival and departure times (2020 on), on the routes that served Somerville at the time: today's routes that ran then, plus any route with a timing stop in Somerville then (routes change: the 86 stopped in Somerville in 2020–22, not in 2026). "Normal" is the same hour on the 4 weeks before.
  • Who walks: Somerville Happiness Survey, "In the past week, which of the following transportation options did you use?" Melt and refreeze: hourly temperature at Logan; "refreezing" means back at or below 32°F within 12 hours after it was 34°F or warmer.
  • Snow in Somerville: CoCoRaHS volunteer observers inside Somerville, via ACIS (24 hours to about 7 a.m.). City page views: City of Somerville website analytics (top 20 pages per day, 2020 to May 2023).
  • Deadline: 6 daylight hours after the snow stops, Sec. 12-8(a), computed with the guide's calculator.
