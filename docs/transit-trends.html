All the numbers
How it's counted
  • Buses: the MBTA's average riders per trip and stop, each fall, added up at stops inside Somerville (placed with that fall's schedule). Friday is folded into the weekday average from 2025.
  • The T: average weekday boardings, Fall 2017–19 and 2023–25. Porter and Sullivan are just outside the city. Lechmere is left out: close to the line, but next to industrial land, not homes.
  • Bluebikes: trips that start or end at a dock in Somerville (once if both), weekdays and weekends of September to November, holidays out. Each station placed at its dock.
  • Who's working: the Census Bureau's yearly survey (usual way to work last week, ± a margin; none for 2020) and its payroll counts (LODES).
  • Boardings, not people: a rider who changes from bus to T counts twice.
  • Somerville since 2015: Assembly opened in 2014, the pandemic hit in 2020, the Green Line Extension opened in 2022, the bus redesign began in Dec 2024.
  • Averages: the MBTA's counts are each fall's average day, from the trips and days it counted.

Sources: MBTA bus ridership by trip, season, route and stop; MBTA rail ridership (2017–2019) and the Fall 2023, Fall 2024 and Fall 2025 rail files; the MBTA schedule archive; Bluebikes system data; American Community Survey; LEHD LODES; MassGIS.
