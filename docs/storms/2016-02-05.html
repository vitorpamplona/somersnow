Sources

  • Weather: hourly observations at Boston Logan (BOS), Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Snowfall: NOAA via ACIS.
  • Requests: Somerville 311 open data.
  • Wards: City of Somerville GIS. Bus riders: MBTA.
  • Warnings: National Weather Service, zone MAZ014, via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet VTEC archive.
  • Tows and disabled vehicles: Somerville police dispatch data (2017 on, per 8-hour shift). Crashes: Somerville police crash reports (2018 on).
  • T entries: MBTA gated station entries at stations in Somerville. MBTA alerts: MBTA service alerts archive (2020 on). Bikes: Bluebikes trip data. "Normal" is the same hour on the 4 weeks before.
  • Deadline: 6 daylight hours after the snow stops, Sec. 12-8(a), computed with the guide's calculator.
