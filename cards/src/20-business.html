SOMERVILLE, MA

Run a business?
Same rules, bigger fines

1
Clear your sidewalk
Including the sidewalk along your parking lot. 3 feet wide, down to the pavement, within 6 daylight hours.
2
The fines are higher
$150 for the first ticket, $300 for each one after.
3
Renting the storefront?
The ordinance covers tenants and occupants as well as owners, so you can be ticketed. Agree with your landlord who shovels before it snows.
4
Clear the curb ramp
If a curb ramp touches your sidewalk, clear it all the way to the crosswalk. Required for businesses since December 2025.
