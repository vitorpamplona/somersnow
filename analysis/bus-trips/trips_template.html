How it's estimated

The MBTA counts, for every scheduled trip, how many people get on and off at each stop (an average of the season's counts). It doesn't say which rider got off where.

So, trip by trip, we assume everyone on the bus is equally likely to get off at the next stop: the people getting off are a mix of everyone on board, in proportion to where they got on. Whoever is still on at the end gets off there. Adding up every trip of the day gives about how many riders go from each stop to each other stop.

It shows where riders leave the bus, not where they end up: many who get off at Davis, Sullivan, Harvard or Lechmere change to the subway. Only these routes are counted, so a trip with a transfer shows as two.

Routes, stops and lines are drawn as they ran in . The ridership file's "Bennett Alley" (a layover before Harvard's upper busway, not a stop in the schedule) is counted at Harvard.
