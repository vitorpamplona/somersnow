# Source files

Original documents the guide was checked against.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `somerville-code-ch12-streets-sidewalks.pdf` | Somerville Code of Ordinances, Chapter 12 (Streets, Sidewalks and Other Public Places), current through Ord. 2025-25 (Sec. 12-8 effective 12/11/2025). Snow rules: Sec. 12-2, 12-8, 12-9, 12-18. |
| `somerville-code-sec-1-11-penalties.pdf` | Somerville Code of Ordinances, Sec. 1-11 (Penalties and violations), effective 9/24/2026. The fine schedule. |
| `ptac-letter-2025-03-25.pdf` | PTAC's letter to Mayor Ballantyne, "Improve Snow Clearing on Somerville Sidewalks" (March 25, 2025), copied to DPW, OSPCD Mobility, ISD and the City Council: 13 recommendations, from treating ice separately to studying City clearing of all sidewalks. |
| `boston-code-ch16.pdf` | City of Boston Code, Chapter XVI (Prohibitions, Penalties and Permits), from American Legal Publishing, saved Nov 2026. Snow: 16-12.16 (sidewalks and curb ramps, 3 hours, 42 in, fines), last amended Ord. 2011 c. 13. |
| `brookline-bylaw-7-7.pdf` | Town of Brookline General By-Laws, Article 7.7 (Removal of Snow and Ice from Sidewalks), as amended Nov 15, 2022 STM Art. 9; downloaded from ecode360 Nov 5, 2026. |
| `cambridge-code-12-16.docx` | Cambridge Municipal Code, Chapter 12.16 (Street and Sidewalk Use Regulations), from Municode, saved Nov 2026. Snow: 12.16.110; fines: 12.16.160; roof leaders: 12.16.070. |
| `minneapolis-code-snow.pdf` | Minneapolis Code of Ordinances, Title 17 (Streets and Sidewalks) chapters, from Municode, saved Nov 2026. Snow: ch. 445 (4 daytime hours, 24 h for 1–2 family homes, City clears and assesses, business licenses), 427.150 (snow into streets); special service districts (e.g., ch. 438) fund snow removal in commercial districts. |
| `madison-mgo-ch10.pdf` | Madison General Ordinances, Chapter 10 (Streets and Sidewalks), from Municode, saved Nov 2026. Snow: 10.23 (snow placed on sidewalks, streets, bike paths), 10.28 (noon the day after, corner curb ramps, salt, City clears as a special charge), 10.29 (downspouts). |
| `nyc-admin-code-16-ch1.pdf` | New York City Administrative Code, Title 16, Chapter 1 (Department of Sanitation), from American Legal Publishing, saved Nov 2026. Snow: 16-123 (owners), 16-124.1 (borough snow plans), 16-135 (volunteer registry). |
| `walkboston-keep-it-clear.pdf` | WalkBoston, *Keep It Clear: Recommendations for Sidewalk Snow and Ice Removal in Massachusetts* (c. 2007). © WalkBoston, included for reference; see [walkboston.org](https://walkboston.org). |

The official, current code is on [Municode](https://library.municode.com/ma/somerville/codes/code_of_ordinances). Excerpts and a plain-language fine table are in [../ordinance.md](../ordinance.md).
