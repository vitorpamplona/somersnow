Made by the Somerville Pedestrian & Transit Advisory Committee (PTAC). Not an official City page; the City publishes neither its list of 281 stops nor its 4 routes. Sources: weekday boardings and alightings by stop, (MBTA open data); stops and routes (MBTA V3 API); route 109 cleared by the MBTA (MBTA); 281 stops in 4 routes (City snow page); ward outlines (City GIS). Built by analysis/bus-stop-clearing, where its README explains how a plan is scored.
