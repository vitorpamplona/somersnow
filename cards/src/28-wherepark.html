SNOW EMERGENCY PARKING
SOMERVILLE, MA

Where
to park

During a snow emergency, you have three choices:
1
The even side
The even-numbered side of your street, unless a sign says otherwise.
2
A City lot
13 lots are open to residents. List at somervillema.gov/offstreetparking
3
Tufts Dowling Garage
Free if you live in Ward 6 or 7. Register with Tufts first.
Never a school lot. The wrong side is a $100 ticket, and you may be towed.
