HOW TO SHOVEL
SOMERVILLE, MA

Get ready
before
it snows

1
Sign up for City Alerts
A call, text or email when a snow emergency starts. somervillema.gov/alerts
2
Get your gear
A sturdy shovel, an ice chopper, and ice melt or sand. Buy ice melt before the first storm. Stores run out.
3
Need help shoveling?
Seniors and people with disabilities can book a teen shoveler before the storm: 617-625-6600 x2321.
